# Home Expense Tracker

So I, you know, I bought a new flat, and then I was renovating it. So I was buying furniture and going to the IKEA, Bauhaus, and buying, you know, a lot of other things to paint the flat. Anyway, to buy all those things, including furniture and everything. So I have a lot of receipts, um, with me, and I want to make like a app or something like that where I can take all these pictures from all these receipts and it automatically, you know, like puts them together in an Excel sheet or in an app or in some kind of format with, you know, putting them nicely. Like, okay, this is from IKEA, and this much it cost, and this is the total bill for that. This is from Bauhaus, and it can also add up in total, you know, based on the date when it was bought or something. Anyway, it can total add up so that I can know how much total I spent, because also for next year when I claim my taxes in Germany, that I can also submit it to tax authorities if they need, you know, if they ask for it, so that I have written down somewhere properly. Also, you know, some of the things I bought on eBay Kleinanzeigen, so, you know, I also paid sometimes cash for such things. So for such things also would be good to have, you know, that are handwritten bill or letter mentioning this is the, this is the number, so that I can also submit those receipts to the tax authority when they ask next year. So I want to build such an app or some kind of, yeah, thing that can track those things and where I can submit this. I can take a picture and it can automatically put everything in the data, because I have all these receipts as paper form and electronic form.
